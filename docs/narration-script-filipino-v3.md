## Notes
- Every `<break>` tag is gone. Pauses now come from `[pause]`, line breaks and "...".
- Tags used are only from your lists: [tired] [sighing] [chuckling] [slowly] [scoffing] [angry] [playful] [excited] [softly] [pause] [keyboard typing] [applause] [crowd cheering].
- I fixed "Walang hasul" to "Walang hassle" and "Maaring" to "Maaaring" so the voice reads them correctly. Change them back if you prefer your original.
- The two sound effects at the end of big moments ([applause], [crowd cheering]) are the riskiest: v3 may add the noise or say it oddly. If it sounds off, delete those two tags and regenerate only that section.
- "PayYuyan" may be read oddly. If so, respell it phonetically as "Pay Yu-yan" for the generation only.
- Keep each generation to one or two paragraphs so a bad tag doesn't cost you a whole re-render.
