Our Story

DiskarTasks started with a simple belief: no talented SLSU student should have to hustle in the dark. We’ve seen how hard our Free-Yuyans work, and how discouraging it can be to have so much to offer yet struggle to reach the people who need it most. DiskarTasks is our way of bringing the campus closer together—giving student freelancers a fair chance to be discovered, and building a community where Yuyans genuinely have each other’s backs.

Our Mission

To empower SLSU students to monetize their skills, build real-world portfolios, and gain practical freelancing experience through a safe, accessible campus marketplace.

Our Vision

To establish a thriving SLSU freelancing ecosystem where every student achieves financial independence, builds a career-ready portfolio, and accesses trusted peer support on demand.

The problem we saw

SLSU students have real skills, yet finding or advertising help usually means scrolling Facebook groups: posts get buried, nobody is verified, there is no order history and no reviews. Word of mouth is trusted but slow, and it is hard to find the right person.

Existing freelancing sites are also marketed for adults and working freelancers, which favours people who already built their profiles and leaves most students out.

How DiskarTasks answers it

  • Trust

    Only SLSU members: sign-up needs an @slsu.edu.ph email.

  • Transparency

    Clear Basic / Standard / Pro packages with price, delivery days, revisions and inclusions.

  • Proof

    Portfolios, ratings and reviews on every seller.

  • Convenience

    In-app chat, order tracking and payment at a campus meet-up.

How DiskarTasks Works

1. Browse

Find the service you need, from logos and websites to tutoring and thesis editing.

2. Connect

Chat with the seller, agree on the details and place your order in a few taps.

3. Get It Done

Meet on campus, receive your work and leave a review to help the next student.

What you can do from your profile

See every order in one place, from In Progress to Completed, with the date, time and package you chose.

Compare packages, delivery days and honest reviews before you decide, then save the gigs you like.

Ask questions and agree on a meet-up time right in the app. Replies show up in your inbox.
